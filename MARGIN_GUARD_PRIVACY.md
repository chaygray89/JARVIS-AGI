# Margin Guard Privacy Notice

**Effective date:** October 1, 2026

Margin Guard is a limited commercial pilot operated by **Chayson Ingram-Gray**.

## What information is used
For business-to-business outreach, Margin Guard may use publicly available or business-contact information such as a person's name, role, employer, business email address, and public professional profile. If a business chooses to participate in a paid pilot, Margin Guard may process the business information the customer voluntarily provides for the audit.

## Purpose
Contact information is used only to evaluate interest in the Margin Guard pilot, communicate about the requested service, administer the pilot, measure aggregate experiment results, and maintain suppression records for people who opt out.

## Data minimization
Margin Guard does not require payment-card data directly. Payments, if enabled, are handled by a separate payment provider. Customer invoice, supplier, or cost records are not accessed unless the customer separately consents and provides them for the pilot.

## Retention
Outreach records are retained only as long as needed for the pilot, legal/compliance records, security, or suppression. If you ask not to be contacted, a minimal suppression record may be retained so the request can be honored.

## Sharing
Margin Guard does not sell outreach contact data. Information may be processed by service providers used for email, payment, hosting, or security only as necessary to operate the pilot.

## Your choices
To opt out of commercial email, reply **unsubscribe** or **do not contact**. The request will be honored and future campaign outreach will be suppressed.

For privacy questions or deletion/correction requests, contact: **aioutreachadmin@gmail.com**.
